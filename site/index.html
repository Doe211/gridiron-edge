How the model works

Every team has a power rating in points. The model predicts each game by subtracting the two ratings and adding a few points for the home team. After every game, both teams' ratings move a little toward what actually happened. Big blowouts are capped so one game doesn't count too much.

In the NFL, the model also gives a small edge to the team with more rest, and takes 2 points off a team starting someone other than its regular quarterback. For the NBA, it predicts total points from each team's offense and defense.

How good is it?

In tests on the 2021–2025 NFL seasons, the model picked the winner about 61–67% of the time. Against the spread it was right about half the time, the same as a coin flip. Betting lines are very hard to beat, so treat the model's picks as an opinion, not a sure thing. The Scorecard shows every league's real record.

Highlighted picks

A pick is highlighted in green when the model disagrees with Vegas by a lot: 3+ points on the spread, 5+ points on an NBA total, or 5+ points of win chance in MLB. That marks a big disagreement, not a better bet.
