Tickets

Build a ticket with 3 to 12 picks (teams against the spread and Over/Under totals) and wager 1 to 100 🪙. Payouts, in 🪙 won for every 🪙 wagered: 3 of 3 pays 5, 4 of 4 pays 10, 5 of 5 pays 20, 6 of 6 pays 35, 7 of 7 pays 60, 8 of 8 pays 100, 9 of 9 pays 150, 10 of 10 pays 200, 11 of 11 pays 300, and 12 of 12 pays 500. A 10-pick ticket also pays 25 for 9 of 10 and 20 for 0 of 10. Otherwise, any losing pick loses the ticket. A push drops that pick and the ticket is graded as the smaller ticket; with fewer than 3 picks left, the wager is returned. Once placed, a ticket can't be changed. 🪙 are points for fun, not money.

Your tickets

Your tickets are saved online under the name you enter, and the site's administrator can see them. The My Tickets tab shows the tickets placed from this device and browser. Each game locks when it starts, and tickets close at the deadline shown on This Week, if there is one.

If you ever bet real money somewhere else and it stops being fun, help is available 24/7 at 1-800-GAMBLER.
