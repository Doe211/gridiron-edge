Disclaimer

Just for fun

Gridiron Edge is a game for amusement only. It's strictly for kicks and giggles. It is not gambling, not a sportsbook, and not betting advice.

No real money

⚾ baseballs are pretend points. They can't be bought, sold, cashed out, or traded for anything. The only thing anyone wins is bragging rights.

Tickets

Build a ticket with 3 to 12 picks (teams against the spread and Over/Under totals) and wager 1 to 100 ⚾.

Ticket picks must be certified an hour before the first game starts, or the ticket is voided.

Minimum Bet: 1 ⚾

“For 1” means ⚾ won for every ⚾ wagered. Otherwise, any losing pick loses the ticket. A push drops that pick and the ticket is graded as the smaller ticket; with fewer than 3 picks left, the wager is returned. Once placed, a ticket can't be changed. ⚾ are points for fun, not money.

Your tickets

Your tickets are saved online under the name you enter, and the site's administrator can see them. The My Tickets tab shows the tickets placed from this device and browser. Each game locks when it starts, and tickets close at the deadline shown on This Week, if there is one.
